# Typhoon CloudVault — Return and Refund Policy

## Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your CloudVault subscription at any time from the Billing section of your account settings or by contacting support. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you will retain access to all features until then.

## Monthly Plans

Monthly subscriptions are eligible for a full refund if cancelled within 7 days of the initial purchase or within 7 days of a plan upgrade. After the 7-day window, no refunds are issued for the current billing period, but you will not be charged again.

## Annual Plans

Annual subscriptions receive a prorated refund for unused months if cancelled within 60 days of purchase. After 60 days, cancellation stops future charges but no refund is issued for the remaining term.

### Example

If you purchased an annual Professional plan ($19/user/month, billed as $228/year) and cancel after 3 months, you would receive a refund of $171 (9 remaining months × $19).

## Free Trial

The 14-day free trial does not require a credit card. If you do not choose a paid plan before the trial ends, your account is automatically downgraded to a read-only state. Your files are preserved for 30 days, after which they are permanently deleted.

## Enterprise Agreements

Enterprise customers with custom contracts should refer to the terms in their Master Service Agreement (MSA). Standard refund policies do not apply to enterprise agreements. Contact your dedicated account manager for any billing adjustments.

## Hardware and Merchandise

Physical items purchased through the CloudVault store (branded USB drives, welcome kits, etc.) can be returned within 30 days of delivery in unused, original condition. Return shipping is covered by Typhoon for defective items. For non-defective returns, the customer is responsible for return shipping costs.

To initiate a hardware return:

1. Log in to your account at app.typhooncloudvault.com
2. Navigate to Orders in account settings
3. Select the order and click "Request Return"
4. Print the prepaid shipping label (defective items) or arrange your own shipping
5. Ship the item within 14 days of the return request

Refunds for physical items are processed within 5-10 business days after we receive the returned item.

## How to Request a Refund

For subscription refunds:

1. Go to **Account Settings → Billing → Subscription**
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**
3. Select **Request Refund** if within the eligible window
4. Confirm the cancellation

Alternatively, contact support at support@typhooncloudvault.com or through the in-app chat. Include your account email and order/subscription ID.

## Processing Time

- Credit card refunds: 5-10 business days
- PayPal refunds: 3-5 business days
- Wire transfer refunds: 10-15 business days

## Disputes

If you believe a charge was made in error, contact support within 30 days of the charge. We aim to resolve all billing disputes within 48 hours.
