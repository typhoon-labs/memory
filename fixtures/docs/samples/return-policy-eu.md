# Typhoon CloudVault — Return and Refund Policy (European Union)

*Applies to consumers residing in the EU/EEA. This policy supplements your statutory rights under EU consumer protection law.*

## Right of Withdrawal

Under EU Directive 2011/83/EU, you have the right to withdraw from your CloudVault subscription within **14 calendar days** of purchase without giving any reason. This applies to all plans purchased online.

To withdraw, send a clear statement to eu-support@typhooncloudvault.com or use **Account Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription → EU Withdrawal**. You may also use the EU model withdrawal form, though it is not required.

Upon valid withdrawal, we reimburse all payments within **14 days** using your original payment method. No fees are charged.

If you requested that the service begin during the withdrawal period, you are liable for a proportionate amount for the service already provided.

## Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your CloudVault subscription at any time from Account Settings or by contacting support. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you retain full access until then. No cancellation fees are charged.

## Monthly Plans

Monthly subscriptions cancelled within 14 days of purchase are covered by the right of withdrawal (see above) and receive a full refund. After 14 days, cancellation stops future charges but no refund is issued for the current billing period.

## Annual Plans

Annual subscriptions receive a **full refund** if cancelled within the 14-day withdrawal period. After the withdrawal period, you receive a prorated refund for unused full months remaining on the contract, minus a reasonable administration fee of EUR 10.

### Example

If you purchased an annual Professional plan (EUR 19/user/month, billed as EUR 228/year) and cancel after 3 months, you would receive EUR 161 (9 remaining months x EUR 19, minus EUR 10 administration fee).

## Free Trial

The 14-day free trial does not require a credit card or any payment details. If you do not choose a paid plan before the trial ends, your account is automatically downgraded to a read-only state. Your files are preserved for **90 days** (extended from 30 days for EU users), after which they are permanently deleted with 14 days prior notice by email.

## Enterprise Agreements

Enterprise customers with custom contracts should refer to the terms in their agreement. Standard refund policies do not apply. Contact your dedicated account manager for billing adjustments.

## Hardware and Merchandise

Physical items purchased through the CloudVault store can be returned within **14 calendar days** of delivery for any reason (EU right of withdrawal). Items must be in original condition. For defective items, you have **2 years** from delivery to request a remedy under the EU statutory warranty (Directive 2019/771).

To initiate a return:

1. Log in at app.typhooncloudvault.com
2. Navigate to **Orders** in account settings
3. Select the order and click **Request Return**
4. Print the prepaid return label — return shipping is always free for EU consumers
5. Ship the item within 14 days

Refunds for physical items are processed within **14 days** of receiving the returned item. We use the same payment method as your original purchase.

## Statutory Warranty

Under EU Directive 2019/771, digital content and services must conform to the contract for a minimum of **2 years**. If CloudVault does not perform as described:

1. We will restore conformity free of charge within a reasonable time
2. If that is not possible, you may request a proportionate price reduction
3. For significant non-conformity, you may terminate the contract and receive a refund

The burden of proof lies with Typhoon for the first **12 months**. In France, the burden remains with Typhoon for the full 2-year period.

## Refund Processing Time

- Credit card refunds: 5-10 business days
- SEPA direct debit: 3-5 business days
- PayPal: 3-5 business days
- Wire transfer: 10-15 business days

All refunds are issued in EUR regardless of the original payment currency, converted at the ECB reference rate on the date of the refund.

## Disputes

If you believe a charge was made in error, contact eu-support@typhooncloudvault.com within 60 days of the charge. We aim to resolve all billing disputes within 48 hours.

If we cannot resolve your complaint, you may:

- Submit a complaint through the EU Online Dispute Resolution platform at https://ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr
- Contact a certified Alternative Dispute Resolution body in your member state
- Contact your national consumer protection authority
